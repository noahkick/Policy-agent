# Vendor Sharing Policy

**Policy ID:** DEMO-VENDOR-SHARING  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 2026-03-01  
**Scope:** Fictional Acme Demo sharing of customer data with external vendors.

## Purpose and Definitions

This fictional policy controls the share action for customer data sent to an external vendor. An **approved vendor** has a current vendor-risk review and a signed data-processing agreement. **Vendor-X** is a named vendor in this demo policy. **Customer data** includes account, transaction, and support records. Sharing means sending, exporting, or granting the vendor access to those records.

## Approved Vendor Rule

Customer data may be shared with an approved vendor only for a documented business purpose and only after the data owner confirms the minimum necessary fields. The share action is allowed for customer data when the vendor is an approved vendor and the business purpose is documented. This rule does not approve a vendor by name; approval must be established by the vendor register.

## Approval Requirement

If the external vendor will process customer data outside the customer's collection region, sharing is conditional on written approval from the Data Protection Officer. The share action is conditional for customer data when the vendor is an approved vendor, the location is cross-border, and required approval is Data Protection Officer. The approval must identify the vendor, destination, fields, and deletion date.

## Explicit Vendor Exception

Vendor-X is not approved for customer data sharing. The share action is denied for customer data when the vendor is Vendor-X, regardless of department or business purpose. Vendor-X may receive synthetic demonstration records that contain no customer data; this document does not classify or authorize any other resource.

## Regional Restriction

Customer data belonging to an EU resident must not be shared with an external vendor whose processing location is outside the EU unless the Data Protection Officer has approved the cross-border transfer and the vendor has a current data-processing agreement. Without both facts, the share action is denied for customer data in the EU cross-border case.

## Unknown Cases

This policy does not decide whether a vendor is approved, whether a purpose is documented, or whether a dataset contains customer data. When those facts are missing, the policy agent must preserve the uncertainty rather than assume approval.
