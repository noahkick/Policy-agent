# Privacy and Data Classification Policy

**Policy ID:** DEMO-PRIVACY-CLASSIFICATION  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 2026-02-15  
**Scope:** region=ALL; department=ALL; vendor=ALL

## Purpose and Definitions

This fictional policy identifies the privacy facts that must accompany decisions about customer data. **Customer data** is classified as Confidential. **EU customer data** is customer data relating to an EU resident. **Synthetic data** is generated data that does not describe a real person. **External sharing** includes sending or granting access to a vendor outside Acme.

## Classification Rule

Customer data must be handled as Confidential and may be used only for a documented business purpose. The resource value customer data refers to account, transaction, and support records. A dataset must not be treated as synthetic merely because it has been sampled or masked; the data owner must confirm its classification.

## EU Handling Rule

EU customer data must remain within approved EU processing locations unless the Data Protection Officer has approved a documented cross-border transfer. The share action is denied for customer data when the location is EU and the destination is an external vendor outside the EU without Data Protection Officer approval. The access and retain actions for EU customer data remain subject to the separate access and retention requirements.

## Purpose and Minimization

Sharing customer data with an external vendor requires a documented purpose and minimum necessary fields. This privacy requirement works together with the Vendor Sharing Policy; it does not establish that a vendor is approved. A request that names no purpose, resource classification, or destination does not contain enough information for a reliable privacy determination.

## Privacy Review Exception

The Data Protection Officer may approve a documented privacy review using a limited customer-data sample when the review has a stated purpose, minimum fields, destination, and deletion date. This exception does not authorize unrestricted production sharing and does not override the Security and Device Policy.

## Unknown Cases

This policy does not define retention periods, employee access roles, device controls, or vendor approval status. It also does not classify datasets that are not described in the request. The policy agent must return UNKNOWN when these facts are needed and are not supplied by another applicable policy.
